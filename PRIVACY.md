# Privacy Policy — Dot Matrix

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Dot Matrix is a single-screen countdown timer. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## Data collection

- Dot Matrix does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
- Dot Matrix does **not** include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- Dot Matrix does **not** require or create an account.
- No network requests are made by the app.

## Data stored locally

- Timer state (such as the active countdown and restoration data) is stored on the device using Android's standard local storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- All of this data remains on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): used to show the running timer notification and the timer completion notification while the app is in the background. You can deny this permission; the timer still works, but background notifications will not be shown.
- **Exact alarms** (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): used to schedule the timer completion alarm so the completion chime can fire at the right time. These are used only for timer functionality.

## Third-party services

Dot Matrix does not use third-party services.

## Children

Dot Matrix is a general-purpose timer utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13.
